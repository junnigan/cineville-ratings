# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Cineville Ratings** — last updated 2026-10-29

## English

Cineville Ratings does not collect, store or sell any personal data, and it has no server of its own.

**What the extension does with data**

- On cineville.nl film pages it reads the film's title, release year, directors and cast from the page.
- To look up ratings, titles, synopses and reviews it sends those film details (not your identity) directly from your browser to: TMDB (api.themoviedb.org), NeoDB (neodb.social), OMDb (omdbapi.com), Douban (movie.douban.com) and IMDb (www.imdb.com). These requests are subject to those services' own privacy policies.
- If you enter a TMDB or OMDb API key, it is stored only in your browser (`chrome.storage.sync`, which Chrome may sync across your own signed-in devices) and is sent only to the matching service.
- Results are cached in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) for up to 7 days. The "Clear cache" button in the settings panel deletes them.
- Review translation uses Chrome's built-in, on-device Translator. Review text is not sent to any server for translation.
- Douban requests are made with your existing Douban login session in the same browser. The extension does not read, store or transmit your Douban credentials.

**What the extension does not do**

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no remote code, and no data sent to the developer.

**Contact**: open an issue at https://github.com/junnigan/cineville-ratings/issues

## 中文

Cineville Ratings 不收集、不存储、不出售任何个人数据,也没有自己的服务器。

**插件如何处理数据**

- 在 cineville.nl 电影页上,插件读取页面里的片名、年份、导演和演员。
- 为了查找评分、译名、简介和短评,插件会把这些电影信息(不含你的身份信息)直接从你的浏览器发送给:TMDB(api.themoviedb.org)、NeoDB(neodb.social)、OMDb(omdbapi.com)、豆瓣(movie.douban.com)和 IMDb(www.imdb.com)。这些请求适用各服务自己的隐私政策。
- 如果你填写了 TMDB 或 OMDb 的 API key,它只保存在你的浏览器里(`chrome.storage.sync`,Chrome 可能会在你自己登录的设备间同步),并且只会发送给对应的服务。
- 查询结果缓存在浏览器本地(`chrome.storage.local`),最多 7 天。设置面板里的"清除缓存"按钮可以删除。
- 短评翻译使用 Chrome 内置的本地翻译功能,短评内容不会发送到任何服务器去翻译。
- 豆瓣请求使用你在同一个浏览器里已有的豆瓣登录状态。插件不会读取、保存或发送你的豆瓣账号信息。

**插件不会做的事**

没有统计分析、没有跟踪、没有广告、没有远程代码,也不会向开发者发送任何数据。

**联系方式**:在 https://github.com/junnigan/cineville-ratings/issues 提交 issue
